This project is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. See `LICENSE`. It moved from version 2 or later on 5 November 2026, so code from GPL-3.0 projects such as WiiCompiled can be used; Dolphin's GPL-2.0-or-later code allows that.

- [Dolphin](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin) (GPL-2.0-or-later) is the reference for how the Wii's hardware behaves, through `docs/hardware_inventory.md`. Any code or tables taken from it must be listed here with the file they came from.
  - Texture sampling (`recomp/runtime/gx_draw.c`, `gx_texture.c`): the rules for which stored mip levels are read, the level of detail clamp where the largest level wins, and wrap mode 3 clamping follow `Source/Core/VideoCommon/RenderState.cpp` (`SamplerState::Generate`) and `TextureInfo.cpp`.
  - Fog (`recomp/runtime/gx_tev.c`, `read_fog` and `add_fog`): the depth to distance formulas, the four curve lines, the range adjustment and the whole-number color mix are taken from `Source/Core/VideoCommon/PixelShaderGen.cpp` (`WriteFog`) and `PixelShaderManager.cpp`; the register layout and the infinite A and C case from `BPMemory.h` and `BPMemory.cpp`.
  - Logic operations (`recomp/runtime/gx_draw.c`, `approximate_logic`, and `read_logic` in `gx_tev.c`): the table of blending that stands in for each operation, the shader's part, and treating a no-op as no writing are taken from `Source/Core/VideoCommon/RenderState.cpp` (`BlendingState::Generate` and `ApproximateLogicOpWithBlending`) and `PixelShaderGen.cpp` (`WriteLogicOpBlend`).
  - Lines and points (`recomp/runtime/gx_draw.c`, `gx_draw_expanded`): widening a line up and down or sideways by its slope in pixels, the sizes in sixths of a pixel, and no culling follow `Source/Core/VideoCommon/ShaderGenCommon.cpp` (`GenerateLineOffset`), `GeometryShaderGen.cpp`, `GeometryShaderManager.cpp` and `RenderState.cpp`.
  - Viewport depth range (`recomp/runtime/gx_draw.c`, `apply_viewport`): the near and far ends worked out from the viewport's z range and far value follow `Source/Core/VideoCommon/BPFunctions.cpp` (`SetScissorAndViewport`).
  - Destination alpha (`recomp/runtime/gx_draw.c`, `gx_copy.c`, `gx_tev.c`): when the constant output alpha applies, how it blends when subtracting or with a no-op, and a pixel format without alpha storing none, blending its alpha as one and copying it as full, follow `Source/Core/VideoCommon/RenderState.cpp` (`BlendingState::Generate`), `PixelShaderGen.cpp`, `BPFunctions.cpp` (`ClearScreen`) and `TextureConverterShaderGen.cpp`.
  - IOS title service (`recomp/runtime/es.c`, `es_titles.c`, `es_content.c`, `titles.c`): the ES request numbers and their buffers, the layout of TMD and ticket views, and the Korean-region check's answer follow `Source/Core/Core/IOS/ES/ES.h`, `Identity.cpp` and `Formats.cpp`.
  - WiiConnect24 and network settings (`recomp/runtime/wc24.c`, `net.c`): the user ID algorithm and its area and model tables are taken from `Source/Core/Core/IOS/Network/KD/NetKDRequest.cpp` (`NWC24MakeUserID`), the config file layout and checksum from `NWC24Config.h` and `NWC24Config.cpp`, the scheduler and settings answers from `NetKDRequest.cpp` and `Network/NCD/Manage.cpp`, and the setting.txt key from `Source/Core/Common/SettingsHandler.cpp`.
  - Attachment encryption (`recomp/runtime/wii_attachment_key.c` and `wii_attachment_tables.h`): making the two tables from the key a game writes, by trying each of the seven indexes and falling back for a key none matches, and the first-party tables themselves, are taken from `Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/Encryption.cpp`, itself based on work by Hector Martin. The tables header is generated from that file by a script.
  - IR camera (`recomp/runtime/wii_camera.c`): the register layout, the tracking switch and the basic and extended dot formats follow `Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/Camera.cpp` and `Camera.h`.
  - Wii MotionPlus (`recomp/runtime/wii_motionplus.c`): its registers and addresses, activating and deactivating and how long each takes, the attachment pin during them, passing an attachment through every other report and the bits that moves, the gyro readings' layout and scales, the calibration blocks and their CRC-32, and the check's steps follow `Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/MotionPlus.cpp` and `MotionPlus.h`. The check's answers (`wii_motionplus_challenge.h`) are worked out by `recomp/tools/motionplus_challenge.py` from that file's modulus and square root, with a starting number of our own.
  - Wii Remote motion and the Nunchuk (`recomp/runtime/wii_remote.c`, `wii_remote_accel_bytes`, and `wii_attachment.c`, `attachment_nunchuk_state`): the zero and one g points, where the motion's lowest bits sit in the button bytes, sending a status report and stopping data reports when an attachment goes in or out, and the Nunchuk's identity, calibration, stick reach and data layout follow `Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/WiimoteEmu.h`, `WiimoteEmu.cpp` (`ProcessExtensionPortEvent`), `Extension/Nunchuk.h`, `Extension/Nunchuk.cpp` and `Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteCommon/DataReport.cpp`, `WiimoteReport.h`.
  - Paired devices (`recomp/runtime/bluetooth.c`, `bluetooth_register_remote`): the layout of the settings' BT.DINF, with the board in the fifth active record, follows `Source/Core/Core/IOS/USB/Bluetooth/BTEmu.cpp` and `BTEmu.h`.
  - Serial interface (`recomp/runtime/si.c`): the communication and status register bits, and an empty port's transfer ending with a communication error and "no response", follow `Source/Core/Core/HW/SI/SI.cpp` and `SI.h`.
  - Disc drive (`recomp/runtime/dvd.c`, `drive.c`): the drive request numbers, the open-partition buffers, and the unencrypted area's layout of partition table and region follow `Source/Core/Core/IOS/DI/DI.cpp` and `Source/Core/DiscIO/VolumeWii.cpp`.
  - NAND import (`recomp/tools/import_nand.py`): the backup's layout with its spare bytes, the SFFS superblock with its cluster chain and file tree, and decrypting each cluster with the console's NAND key follow `Source/Core/DiscIO/NANDImporter.cpp`.
  - Audio processor (`recomp/runtime/dsp.c`, `hardware.c`, `ax.c`): the mail queue and its interrupt rules follow `Source/Core/Core/HW/DSPHLE/MailHandler.cpp`; the control register's split between the processor's bits and the interrupt flags, and what reset, halt and init do, follow `DSPHLE.cpp` and `Source/Core/Core/HW/DSP.cpp`; the boot ROM, start-up and memory card programs follow `DSPHLE/UCodes/ROM.cpp`, `INIT.cpp` and `CARD.cpp`; the task mails, the ten mails that describe a new program and swapping programs follow `UCodes.h` and `UCodes.cpp`; the table of programs by checksum is taken from `UCodes.cpp` (`UCodeFactory`), `ASnd.h` and `AESnd.h`, and the checksum itself from `Source/Core/Common/Hash.cpp` (`HashEctor`).
  - AX audio programs (`recomp/runtime/ax.c`, `ax_voice.h`): ported from `Source/Core/Core/HW/DSPHLE/UCodes/AX.cpp`, `AX.h`, `AXWii.cpp`, `AXWii.h`, `AXVoice.h` and `AXStructs.h`: the GameCube and Wii command lists, the voice block layouts, sample-rate conversion, filters, mixing, aux, compressor and output, and the mail protocol (`HandleMail`, `SignalWorkEnd`). Its tests (`test/c/test_ax.c`) work their expected values out from the same files.
  - Audio processor interpreter (`recomp/runtime/dsp_lle.c`, `dsp_lle_ops.c`, `dsp_lle.h`): ported from `Source/Core/Core/DSP/DSPCore.cpp`, `DSPHWInterface.cpp`, `DSPMemoryMap.cpp`, `DSPStacks.cpp`, `DSPTables.cpp`, `DSPAnalyzer.cpp` (its loop ends and mail-wait patterns only) and `DSP/Interpreter/*`, with the control register and mailboxes from `Source/Core/Core/HW/DSPLLE/DSPLLE.cpp` and `DSPHost.cpp`. The accelerator (`recomp/runtime/dsp_accelerator.c`) is ported from `Source/Core/Core/DSP/DSPAccelerator.cpp`, with the Wii's memory routing from `HW/DSP.cpp` (`ReadARAM`, `WriteARAM`). Its tests (`test/c/test_dsp_lle.c`) port `Source/UnitTests/Core/DSP/DSPAcceleratorTest.cpp` and run `DSPTestBinary.cpp`'s program.
  - Free audio processor ROM (`recomp/runtime/data/dsp_rom.bin`, `dsp_coef.bin`): Dolphin's free replacement boot ROM and filter table, v0.4, from `Data/Sys/GC` (see `docs/DSP/free_dsp_rom/dsp_rom_readme.txt`), byte for byte.
  - Zelda-family audio program (`recomp/runtime/zelda.c`): ported from `Source/Core/Core/HW/DSPHLE/UCodes/Zelda.cpp` and `Zelda.h`, with the GameCube link's key exchange from `UCodes/GBA.cpp` (`ProcessGBACrypto`).
  - New-Wii files (`recomp/runtime/system_files.c`): the system settings file's layout and a new Wii's items and values follow `Source/Core/Core/SysConf.cpp`. The WiiConnect24 settings' layout, its default servers and its checksum, and the version, sizes and entry counts of the friend list, download list and mailbox indexes follow `Source/Core/Core/IOS/Network/KD/NWC24Config.cpp`, `NWC24DL.h`, `Mail/WC24FriendList.h` and `Mail/WC24Send.h`. The empty mailboxes and indexes were checked byte for byte against ones a Wii Menu system update made in Dolphin.
  - Real Wii Remotes (`recomp/runtime/remote_link.c`, `remote_link_linux.c`, `remote_link_mac.c`): reaching a remote over raw L2CAP sockets on PSM 0x11 and 0x13, searching with the LAP for devices in pairing mode, and telling the board from a remote by its attachment ID at 0xa400fe follow `Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteReal/IOLinux.cpp` and `WiimoteReal.cpp` (`IsBalanceBoard`). Using IOKit's HID on the Mac, and the kernel's hidraw devices on Linux (`remote_link_hidraw.c`), follows Dolphin's move to `IOhidapi.cpp`.
  - Picture copies in memory, EFB peeks and pokes, and the bounding box (`recomp/runtime/gx_encode.c`, `gx_copy.c`, `gx_efb.c`, the bounding box in `gx_tev.c`): the order of the copy's steps (the picture's pixel format, halving, the vertical filter on color only with its three summed weights and its wrap past 128, gamma, then YUV with its constants and rounding), each format's bytes, and depth's three bytes as red, green and blue follow `Source/Core/VideoCommon/TextureConversionShader.cpp` (`WriteSampleFunction` and the encoders) and `TextureCacheBase.cpp` (`GetRAMCopyFilterCoefficients`, `CopyFilterCanOverflow`); the formats Dolphin's software encoder has no answer for, named instead, from `Source/Core/VideoBackends/Software/TextureEncoder.cpp`; intensity needing bits 15 and 16 from `BPStructs.cpp` and `BPMemory.h`; a peek's or poke's address, color order, pixel format and alpha read mode from `Source/Core/Core/PowerPC/MMU.cpp` (`EFB_Read`, `EFB_Write`) and `Source/Core/VideoCommon/EFBInterface.cpp`; 16-bit depth from `VideoCommon.h` (`CompressZ16`); and the bounding box's registers, its squares of 2 by 2 and turning off when read or at a copy to the screen from `PixelEngine.cpp`, `BPStructs.cpp` and `PixelShaderGen.cpp` (`UpdateBoundingBox`).
  - Video interface timing (`recomp/runtime/vi.c`): the timing registers' fields, a field's half-lines (`3 EQU + PRB + 2 ACV + PSB`), samples per half-line and the clock select, the beam counting half-lines across a frame, and when each display interrupt fires, follow `Source/Core/Core/HW/VideoInterface.cpp` and `VideoInterface.h` (`GetTicksPerHalfLine`, `GetHalfLinesPerEvenField`, `Update`); the component cable bit from `VideoInterface.h`.
  - Power and reset (`recomp/runtime/stm.c`): the request numbers, and shutdown and idle turning the console off, follow `Source/Core/Core/IOS/STM/STM.h` and `STM.cpp`.
  - Storage numbers (`recomp/runtime/fs.c`, `write_stats` and `get_usage`): the cluster size, the clusters and inodes a NAND has and keeps for IOS, and counting a folder's use follow `Source/Core/Core/IOS/FS/FileSystem.h` and `HostBackend/FS.cpp` (`GetNandStats`, `GetDirectoryStats`).
  - The clock (`recomp/runtime/exi.c`): leaving the settings' counter bias out of the real-time clock follows `Source/Core/Core/HW/EXI/EXI_DeviceIPL.cpp`.
- [WiiCompiled](https://github.com/patchzyy/Wiicompiled) (GPL-3.0). Any code taken from it must be listed here with the file it came from, as for Dolphin. Before the move to version 3 it showed two designs we built our own versions of, with none of its code: passing a physical Wii Remote's reports straight to the game's own Bluetooth code, and a flat guest memory at a fixed host address.
  - Write watching (`recomp/runtime/guest_memory.c`, `memory_watch.h`): a count of writes for each piece of guest memory, which a reader compares to tell that what it read is unchanged, follows `runtime/include/gx_guest_write.h` (`GenerationForRange`, `NotifyWrite`). WiiCompiled counts the game's cache flushes and DMA; here a watched page is made read-only and its first write is counted as it faults, so no flush is needed.
  - Register write counts (`recomp/runtime/gx_vertex.c`, `gx_draw.c`, `gx_transform.c`; `gx_cp_written`, `gx_settings_written`, `gx_xf_written`): working a vertex format, a draw's stages and shader, and the matrix blocks out once and keeping them until the game writes what they come from follows the vertex state generation in `runtime/src/hle/gx/gx_dl.cpp` (`vtxStateGeneration`).
- Hermes (BSD-3-Clause): the libasnd sound mixer program that `test/c/test_dsp_lle.c` runs, as Dolphin's `Source/UnitTests/Core/DSP/HermesBinary.cpp` carries it. Its notice is kept in `test/c/dsp_lle_programs.h`.
